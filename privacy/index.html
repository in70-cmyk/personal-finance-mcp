Privacy Policy

Personal Finance MCP · Effective 15 October 2026

Purpose and scope

This project is intended solely for its operator’s private, non-commercial use with their own bank accounts. It is not a public banking service and does not accept other users.

Bank data and consent

When configured and explicitly authorised, the application may retrieve account identifiers, account details, balances and transactions through Enable Banking and the selected bank. Access is read-only and limited to the permissions granted. The application does not initiate payments. Bank authentication takes place through the bank’s authorisation process; bank passwords and authentication codes must not be collected by this project.

Use, storage and sharing

Bank data is used only for personal financial review and analysis. It must be kept in the operator’s private environment, retained only as needed, and never committed to this public repository or published on this website. API keys, private keys, tokens and consent identifiers must also remain private. Bank data is not sold or used for advertising.

If the operator connects an MCP client or AI service, any data sent to that service is subject to its own privacy terms. Such connections should be configured to share only data the operator intends to disclose. Enable Banking, the bank and any chosen service process data under their respective privacy notices.

Control and deletion

The operator can stop using the application, revoke access through available bank or Enable Banking controls, and delete locally retained data and credentials. Revocation does not automatically delete copies already held by other services.

This website

These static documentation pages contain no bank data, forms, advertising or project-added analytics or cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process visitor information, such as IP addresses and request logs, under GitHub’s privacy statement.

This policy describes the intended operating restrictions. Any future change in use or data handling requires a corresponding review and update.
